What's inside WalletLens

Import a portfolio from a screenshot

Screenshot any exchange, broker or wallet app, even a handwritten list, and WalletLens reads every row into holdings with AI. It works where there is no API, and never asks for a login.

Add holdings by voice, in English or Arabic

Choose a language, tap the mic and say what you bought. WalletLens understands several trades in one sentence and fills in asset, amount, price and date.

Every asset in one net worth

Property, stocks and ETFs, gold and silver by weight, cash in any currency, bonds and 10,000+ coins, in one live figure broken out by asset class.

Six themes

Emerald, Gold, Silver, Bitcoin, Ethereum and Solana, each with its own glow, accent and watermark behind your balance.

An app that reacts to your day

A greeting that reads your day, a rocket when you are up more than 5%, fireworks on a new all-time high, and a calm view with your runway on red days.

Technical analysis with levels

An EMA 21/55/200 cross with an RSI filter, buy and sell signals on the chart, an ATR stop and three take-profit targets for any coin, stock or metal.

Smart money flow

See whether smart-money wallets are buying or selling each coin you hold, over 24 hours, 7 days and 30 days, powered by Nansen.

Smart sell plan

One tap builds an exit plan for every holding from support, resistance, RSI, MACD and trend: trim, hold, take-profit targets or a trailing stop.

Zakat calculator

Nisab priced from live gold and silver, the zakat due worked out from your real holdings, and your zakat year tracked on the Hijri calendar.

Portfolio Guardian

If you stop opening WalletLens for the interval you choose, your chosen heirs receive your message and a QR of your holdings. You are always warned first.

Backup and restore

A backup code or QR restores everything on a new phone, and an automatic encrypted backup can go to your own Google Drive.

AI coach and growth path

"What should I do right now?" gives you a verdict on your portfolio, and Grow My Net Worth projects when you are likely to reach your goal.

Backup

Backed up to your own Google Drive.

Connect Google Drive and WalletLens keeps an encrypted copy of your portfolio there, updated as it changes. Only you hold the passphrase. New phone? Connect again and everything comes back.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is WalletLens?

WalletLens is a free net worth tracker for crypto, stocks, ETFs, gold, silver, property and cash. It works on the web, on Android and as a Chrome extension, without an account.

How do I import my portfolio from a screenshot?

Take a screenshot of your holdings in any broker, exchange or wallet app, even a handwritten list, and drop it into WalletLens. AI reads every row into a holding you check and save. No CSV and no API keys.

Can I add holdings by voice?

Yes, in English or Arabic. Tap the mic and say what you bought, for example "bought 2 Solana and 3 Cardano", and WalletLens fills in the asset, amount, price and date. One sentence can hold several trades.

Does WalletLens calculate zakat?

Yes. It checks your live portfolio against a nisab priced from current gold and silver, works out the 2.5% due, and tracks your zakat year on the Hijri calendar.

What is Portfolio Guardian?

A legacy switch. You choose an interval of 30, 90 or 180 days and up to three people. Opening the app resets the countdown. If it ever runs out you are emailed first, and only if you do not respond do your heirs receive your message and a QR of your holdings.

Does WalletLens have technical analysis?

Yes. Every coin, stock and metal gets an EMA 21/55/200 cross with an RSI filter, buy and sell signals, an ATR stop and three take-profit targets, plus a one-tap smart sell plan.

Is WalletLens on Android, Windows and Chrome?

Yes. It is on Google Play for Android, on the Microsoft Store for Windows, as a Chrome extension that shows your net worth from the toolbar, and on the web at walletlens.live.

What languages is WalletLens available in?

The app is available in six languages: English, Arabic with full right-to-left layout, French, Spanish, German and Italian. Voice import understands English and Arabic.

Is WalletLens free?

Yes. There is no paid tier, no holding limit and no feature behind a paywall.
