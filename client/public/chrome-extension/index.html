What is the WalletLens Chrome extension?

The WalletLens Chrome extension is a free net worth and portfolio tracker that lives in your browser toolbar. One click shows your total net worth across crypto, stocks, ETFs, gold, silver, cash, bonds and property, the 24 hour change, profit and loss per asset, market data, technical signals and news. It syncs on its own with the WalletLens app at walletlens.live, needs no account, never connects to an exchange or bank, and keeps your holdings inside your own browser.

Features

Everything you own, in your toolbar

Most portfolio extensions only follow coin prices. WalletLens follows your actual net worth, every asset you own, and keeps it current while you browse.

Live net worth and 24h change

One total across every account and asset, updated with live prices, plus the move in the last 24 hours in money and percent.

Profit and loss per asset

Real coin and company logos, the value of each holding and how it is doing, with your top holdings first and the full list a tab away.

Crypto, stocks, gold and property

Bitcoin and 10,000 other coins, stocks and ETFs, gold and silver, cash, bonds and your home. If it has a value, it counts.

Signals and technical analysis

A TA tab with a clear read per holding, plus the market overview and the Fear and Greed index, so you know the mood before you act.

Market news in the popup

Headlines that move markets, without opening another tab or leaving the page you are on.

Syncs on its own

Add a trade on walletlens.live and the toolbar updates. It keeps working with the site closed, and a backup code brings in your phone's portfolio.

Share performance, not your balance

Post your return as a percentage to X, WhatsApp or Telegram. The amount you hold stays private.

Screenshot and voice import

Build your portfolio in WalletLens from a screenshot of any app or by voice, and it shows up in the toolbar.

Nothing to connect

No exchange API keys, no wallet signatures, no bank logins. Nothing is linked, so nothing can leak.

How to install the WalletLens Chrome extension

Five steps, no sign up. The same steps work in Edge, Brave, Opera and Arc.

  1. Open the listing

    Go to WalletLens on the Chrome Web Store.

  2. Add it

    Click Add to Chrome, then Add extension. No account and no personal details.

  3. Pin it

    Click the puzzle icon by the address bar and pin WalletLens to the toolbar.

  4. Open WalletLens once

    Visit walletlens.live and add your holdings by screenshot, voice or by hand.

  5. Click the icon

    Your net worth, holdings, market data and news, whenever you want them.

Private by design

Your portfolio stays in your browser

Most free trackers are free because your data is the product. WalletLens never sees your balances, so there is nothing to sell. The extension is open source, so anyone can check.

  • No account. No email, no password, nothing to breach.
  • Local storage only. Your holdings are kept in your browser's extension storage.
  • No analytics or telemetry inside the extension.
  • Market data only: prices, exchange rates, news and the Fear and Greed index are fetched by asset symbol. Never your amounts, balances or addresses.
  • Narrow permissions: storage, walletlens.live and public market data services. It cannot read the other sites you visit.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is the WalletLens Chrome extension free?

Yes. The extension and the WalletLens app are free, with no paid tier, no holding limit and no ads inside the extension.

Do I need an account to use the extension?

No. There is no sign up, no email and no password. The extension reads your portfolio from walletlens.live in the same browser and keeps a copy in your browser's own extension storage.

Which browsers does the WalletLens extension work in?

Any Chromium browser that installs from the Chrome Web Store: Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi and Arc, on Windows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOS. Firefox and Safari are not supported; use walletlens.live there instead.

What can I track with the extension?

Everything you track in WalletLens: over 10,000 cryptocurrencies, stocks and ETFs, gold and silver, cash, bonds, property and any other asset you record. The toolbar shows them all as one net worth number with the 24 hour change.

Is my portfolio data private?

Yes. Your holdings stay in your browser. The extension has no analytics or telemetry. Its only outside requests fetch public prices, exchange rates, news and the Fear and Greed index, and they carry asset symbols, never your balances, amounts or addresses.

Does the extension work when walletlens.live is closed?

Yes. It saves your portfolio to extension storage and fetches live prices each time you open it, so the toolbar stays current even with no WalletLens tab open.

Do I have to connect an exchange, wallet or bank?

No. Nothing is connected: no exchange API key, no wallet signature and no bank login. You add holdings in WalletLens by screenshot, by voice in English or Arabic, from a spreadsheet or by hand.

How do I sync the extension with my phone?

Create a backup code in WalletLens on your phone and paste it into the extension with the import button. Your whole portfolio appears in the toolbar in seconds, with no account in between.

What permissions does the extension ask for?

Storage, to keep your portfolio in the browser, and access to walletlens.live plus a short list of public price and market data services such as CoinGecko, Stooq and gold-api. It cannot read the other websites you visit.
