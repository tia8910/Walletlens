## How to Use These Templates

1. **Sign up for HARO** (https://www.helpareporter.com/) or **Connectively** (https://connectively.com/)
2. **Set up alerts** for: finance, crypto, privacy, fintech, personal finance, investing
3. **Respond within 1 hour** of receiving a query (journalists have tight deadlines)
4. **Personalize** the response — reference the journalist's specific article/topic
5. **Include**: name, title, credentials, quote-ready text, website, social handles
