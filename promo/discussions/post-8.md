## The Magic Indicator

Our flagship feature fuses **5 different signal types** into one direction per holding:

### 1. Technical Signals
- RSI (Relative Strength Index)
- MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)
- Bollinger Bands
- Moving Averages (SMA/EMA)

### 2. Volume Signals
- Volume profile analysis
- On-Balance Volume
- Volume-Price Trend

### 3. On-Chain Signals (Crypto)
- Active addresses
- Exchange flows
- Hash rate trends

### 4. Whale Activity
- Large transaction detection
- Exchange inflow/outflow patterns

### 5. Fundamental Signals
- Market cap dominance
- Network value to transactions ratio
- Stock earnings / Gold macro factors

---

## Category-Specific Analysis

### Crypto
All 5 signal types above apply.

### Stocks
- Technical + Volume + Fundamental
- Earnings data, P/E ratio, dividend yield
- Sector correlation

### Gold/Silver
- Technical + Volume
- Inflation correlation
- Dollar index relationship
- Central bank buying trends
