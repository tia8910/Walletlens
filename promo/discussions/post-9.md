We built a **Zakat Calculator** directly into WalletLens because no privacy-first app offered this.

---

## How It Works

1. Your portfolio total is calculated automatically
2. The app checks the current **Nisab threshold** using live gold/silver prices
3. If your total exceeds Nisab, **2.5% Zakat** is calculated
4. You can project the amount in any currency

---

## Features

- Live gold and silver price feeds
- Multi-currency support (USD, EUR, GBP, SAR, AED, etc.)
- One-tap Zakat amount calculation
- Shareable results (local only — no data leaves your device)

---

## Privacy

- Your portfolio data stays on your device
- No financial data is sent to any server
- The calculation happens entirely in your browser/app

---

## Access

1. Open WalletLens
2. Tap the hamburger menu (☰)
3. Select **Zakat Calculator**
