## What is Portfolio Guardian?

Portfolio Guardian is WalletLens' built-in AI safety system that watches your holdings 24/7 and alerts you to problems before they become losses.

---

### 🔍 Anomaly Detection
Portfolio Guardian learns your normal portfolio patterns and flags when something unusual happens:
- Sudden volatility spikes
- Unusual price movements
- Abnormal volume patterns

### ⚖️ Concentration Risk
- Warns when a single asset grows too large in your portfolio
- Suggests rebalancing targets based on your risk profile
- Tracks your diversification score over time

### 📉 Drawdown Alerts
- Monitors unrealized losses in real-time
- Alert thresholds configurable per asset class
- Crypto, stocks, gold, silver each have different risk parameters

---

## Why It Matters

Most portfolio trackers show you numbers. Portfolio Guardian **interprets** them and tells you what to do.

> "Your portfolio volatility has increased 3x this week"
> "You are 80% concentrated in one asset — that is higher risk than your usual profile"
