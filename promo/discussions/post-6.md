## Quick Start

### 1. Open the App
No account needed. Just open [walletlens.live](https://walletlens.live) or install from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=live.walletlens).

### 2. Add Your First Asset
- Tap the **+** button on the dashboard
- Search for any crypto, stock, or commodity
- Enter your holding amount and purchase price

### 3. Explore the Dashboard
- **Net Worth** — Your total portfolio value in real-time
- **P&L** — Profit and loss for each holding and total
- **Allocation** — Visual breakdown of your portfolio
- **Magic Indicator** — AI direction signal for each asset

## Key Features

### 🛡️ Portfolio Guardian
Enable in Settings. Monitors your portfolio for risk and anomalies.

### 🔔 Push Notifications
Allow notifications when prompted. You will get:
- Price alerts (5%+ movement)
- Risk alerts from Guardian
- Feature tips and investment hacks

### 🧠 AI Coach
Ask questions like:
- "Should I buy more Bitcoin?"
- "What is my risk level?"
- "How is my gold performing?"

### 💰 Zakat Calculator
Open from the hamburger menu. Auto-calculates 2.5% Nisab.

### 📊 Technical Analysis
Go to Analysis tab for:
- RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands
- On-chain metrics (crypto)
- Volume analysis
- Magic Indicator composite score
