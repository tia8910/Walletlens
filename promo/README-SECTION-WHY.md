## 💡 Why I Built This

I tried every portfolio tracker out there. They all had the same problems:

1. **Account required** — I just want to track my money, not create another account
2. **Data on their servers** — My financial data sitting on someone else's database
3. **Analytics everywhere** — They know exactly what I'm looking at
4. **Freemium traps** — Basic features locked behind $10/month

So I built WalletLens. It's free, open source, and your data never leaves your device.

**If this helps you, a star helps others find it.** ⭐
